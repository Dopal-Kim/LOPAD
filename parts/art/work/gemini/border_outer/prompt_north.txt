TASK: paint ONE very wide horizontal strip that is the NORTH BORDER of a top-down action-game arena in the outer back streets of the liquor city "Zan" at night. The arena floor (an open cobbled square) lies just BELOW this image and is not painted here.
CONTENT (left to right, one continuous street front): a row of tall, crowded 2- to 3-storey half-timbered houses standing shoulder to shoulder, their FRONT walls facing the viewer, seen from slightly above so the front walls are fully visible and the roofs slope back and up. Upper floors jut out over lower floors on carved wooden brackets; the houses lean against each other at slightly different heights. Include: a low tavern front with a glowing open doorway and a hanging tankard-shaped sign on an iron bracket (no letters); several shuttered windows, a few lit warm amber, most dark; a dark narrow alley gap with an archway between two houses; laundry lines with hanging rags strung between upper windows across the gaps; a couple of hanging iron lanterns; barrels, crates and sacks pushed tight against the bottom of the walls (low, not taller than a third of a door).
DEPTH: behind this front row, more roofs, gables and chimneys rise in two or three layers, each layer darker, smaller and hazier with distance, with chimney smoke - so the street front feels tall and the city deep. A dark overcast night sky only in the top tenth of the image.
GROUND LINE: the bottom of the house fronts sits on a perfectly straight, horizontal ground line at the very bottom edge of the image (a thin strip of dark wet cobbles at most). Nothing sticks out below it.
TILING: the strip will repeat horizontally - make the far left and far right ends similar plain dark plaster walls at the same height so they can be joined.
