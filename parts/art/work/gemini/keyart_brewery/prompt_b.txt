SCENE: The brewery and distillery district deep inside the liquor empire "Zan" - an industrial yard at night, outdoors under a smoky sky. In the center, a stone canal full of liquor runs from the foreground into the distance; the liquor itself glows warm amber and reflects the furnace light - the main light source and focal path of the image. Huge dark iron pot stills and boilers on both sides with open furnace doors glowing amber beneath them, tangled pipes and valves dripping, wooden gantries and cranes hauling barrels on chains. Enormous oak casks stacked in pyramids along the canal banks. Steam rising, lit amber from below. Small hunched workers pushing barrels give scale. Grimy, hot, oppressive.
