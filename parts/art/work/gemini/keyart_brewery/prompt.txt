SCENE: The brewery and distillery district deep inside the liquor empire "Zan" - an industrial yard at night. Towering pot stills and boilers (dark iron and soot gray with small amber glints), tangled pipes and valves, furnaces glowing under the stills. Enormous oak casks stacked in pyramids, wooden gantries, cranes, chains and pulleys hauling barrels. A stone canal of liquor runs through the yard toward the viewer, its surface catching amber light. Steam and vapor drift between the structures. Small hunched workers pushing barrels give scale. Open sky with smoke above (outdoors).
