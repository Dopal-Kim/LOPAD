SCENE: The great gate of the liquor empire "Zan", seen from the muddy approach road at dusk. A massive fortified gatehouse in a high, grim stone curtain wall; the iron portcullis is half raised and warm amber lantern light spills out of the gate tunnel onto the road - the brightest area of the image. Tattered banners with a goblet emblem hang from the battlements, torches on the towers. A wooden drawbridge over a moat of black water. On the road: a ragged queue of refugees and wagons loaded with oak barrels waiting to enter, a toll table with a lantern, and slouching, half-drunk gate guards with halberds and matchlock muskets. The road leads from the viewer straight into the gate, pulling the eye inward.
