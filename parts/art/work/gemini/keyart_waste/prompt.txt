SCENE: The endless battlefield wasteland outside the liquor empire "Zan". Cracked dry earth and churned mud at dusk, a low heavy overcast sky. Broken pikes and swords stuck in the ground at angles, torn banners hanging from leaning poles, dented helmets and shields of fallen soldiers half-buried, a toppled supply cart with one broken wheel. Small ember-colored will-o'-the-wisp soul flames drift up from the fallen and gather toward the center-left, where a faint human silhouette is forming out of the gathered soul fire - the only bright point near the ground. Far on the right horizon, the dark walls and towers of a walled city-empire with a few tiny amber window lights and thin smoke columns, hinting at the destination.
