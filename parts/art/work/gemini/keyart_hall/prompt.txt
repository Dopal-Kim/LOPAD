SCENE: The banquet hall of the ruler of "Zan", at the deepest heart of the liquor empire - an open-air banquet terrace at night under a dark sky with the city rooftops far below. Long banquet tables wrecked and soaked with spilled liquor, overturned goblets and bottles, guttering candelabras, heavy drapes with a goblet emblem. Revelers and servants passed out on the floor. At the far end, on a throne built from stacked casks, the enormous dark silhouette of the ruler: a huge, heavy, utterly drunk lord slumped sideways on the throne, one arm raising a giant goblet that spills liquor, faintly glowing amber eyes. Menacing and grotesque, yet darkly comic. The tables and floor lines lead the eye to the ruler.
