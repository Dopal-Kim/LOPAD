SCENE: The banquet hall of the ruler of "Zan", the deepest heart of the liquor empire - an open-air banquet terrace at night under a dark smoky sky, with the city rooftops far below beyond a stone balustrade (outdoors, not a closed room). Symmetric, centered composition: a long wrecked banquet table runs from the foreground straight toward the center, soaked with spilled liquor, overturned goblets and bottles, guttering candelabras. At the center back, filling about half the image height, the ruler sits on a tall throne built from stacked oak casks: a huge, heavy, utterly drunk lord, slumped and swaying, crown askew, one arm lifting a giant goblet that sloshes amber liquor down over him, glowing amber eyes, a satisfied, dangerous grin. He is a dark silhouette rim-lit from behind by a big brazier fire, so his outline reads clearly against the glow - the focal point of the whole image. Revelers and servants lie passed out around the throne steps. Heavy drapes with a goblet emblem frame both sides. Menacing and grotesque, yet darkly comic.
